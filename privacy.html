PRIVACY & DATA

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Privacy Policy

Global Hope Relief Foundation respects the privacy of people who visit this website or contact us. This policy explains what information may be collected through the Funding & Partnership Interest Form and how it is used.

1. Information you provide

If you submit the enquiry form, you may provide your name, email address, country, the type of person or organisation making the enquiry, your support interest, an optional indicative amount, an optional message and your consent to be contacted.

2. Why we use it

We use the information to receive, review and respond to your funding or partnership enquiry, communicate with you about that enquiry and maintain a basic record of correspondence. The form is an expression-of-interest form only. It does not accept donations or payment-card or bank-account details.

3. Form service provider

The enquiry form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. Information you submit is transmitted to that service and forwarded to the Foundation's contact email. FormSubmit may process information under its own terms and privacy practices. Please review FormSubmit's website before submitting. We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers.

4. External services and links

This website loads some fonts from Google Fonts and may display images hosted by third-party image providers. When your browser requests those resources, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. External websites have their own privacy policies and security practices.

5. Data minimisation and security

Please do not include passwords, bank details, payment-card numbers, government identity numbers, medical information or other sensitive personal information in the enquiry form. We take reasonable steps within our control to protect correspondence, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not sell enquiry information. It may be handled by service providers needed to deliver the form or email, or disclosed where required by law. We retain correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain organisational records and meet applicable obligations. The retention practices of third-party providers are governed by their own policies.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. We may need enough information to identify the enquiry safely.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact foundationglobalhoperelief@gmail.com.

Important: This policy is a practical website notice, not legal advice. It should be reviewed as the Foundation's operations, legal status and data-handling arrangements develop.
