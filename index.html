Who we are

Building a foundation for meaningful help

Global Hope Relief Foundation is currently an early-stage humanitarian initiative led by its founder, MD NUR ALAM MIA. We are working to establish a responsible foundation built on honesty, dignity, transparency and accountability.

We do not claim to have a large team, district offices or active field projects at this stage. As resources and the required approvals become available, we plan to recruit and verify trusted volunteers and expand our work step by step.

Our intended areas of focus

Where we hope to help

Food support

Support for families experiencing food insecurity.

Education

Helping children and learners access educational essentials.

Health and essentials

Exploring responsible ways to support basic health and clothing needs.

Emergency relief

Preparing for future, properly approved disaster-response activities.

These are planned areas of work, not a statement that projects are currently operating.

Trust and accountability

Honesty comes first

  • We are not yet a legally registered NGO.
  • We do not publish invented beneficiary numbers, project results, staff lists or financial figures.
  • No donation payment facility is currently provided through this website.
  • Any future foreign-funded voluntary activity will proceed only after the applicable registration, permissions and project approvals are obtained.
  • We intend to publish clear project and financial updates when lawful activities begin.

Visitors and potential supporters are welcome to contact us for clarification about our current status.
